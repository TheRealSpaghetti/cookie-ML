# Cookie Run Classic (คุกกี้รัน คลาสสิค, com.devsisters.crg): how Thai players farm XP to level 110 (Thai-language sources)

Research date: 2026-10-12. About 25 tool calls. All sources are Thai-language unless marked otherwise.

Source-quality caveat (applies to everything below): almost every Thai page that ranks for these queries is commercial. They are top-up shops (24buym, indexgame, Richman/OverTopup sponsors), account and service sellers (499K Network, JOHNNY SHOP, item2you, khomkhom), bot or cheat sellers (cookiegamebot, botcookierun/NNAN Shop, cookierunbot, tavisshop), or the LDPlayer emulator blog. I found **no Pantip thread** about Classic 2026 leveling; the Pantip ban threads that came up date from the old LINE Cookie Run era. Search results did not surface any **Facebook group posts**. TikTok discover pages and the YouTube body/transcripts could not be fetched; YouTube auto-caption downloads came back empty. Only YouTube **titles, channels, dates, view counts and descriptions** were retrievable, so no video content (actual per-run numbers shown on screen) has been verified.

## 1. What is the fastest XP route Thai players report, and how long did reaching 110 take?

### Takeaway
The consensus Thai route is AFK "Auto Run" farming: a cookie/pet/treasure set that runs on its own. Players run short Episode 1 runs over and over, or long runs with Fast Start and Relay revives, stack EXP-% treasures, and take the daily EXP x2 from the Lab event. **No Thai source I found reports how long it took to reach level 110.** The only time claim is a sales page's "levels 1-50 in 1 week", which the page's own content does not support.

### Cited Findings
- The LDPlayer TH blog (author "akira", 2026-07-14) recommends the "Auto Run EXP" method. It farms **Episode 1 (ด่านที่ 1)** as the fastest option and quits once the run reaches stage 2: "พอวิ่งจนถึงด่าน 2 ให้กดออก" ("when you reach stage 2, quit"). Each game takes about 2 minutes ("เกมละ 2 นาที"), then you restart. The post also says to use "Fast Start แล้วเริ่มใหม่". It insists Auto Run is not a cheat: "Autorun ไม่เท่ากับ Auto Click!!!" ("Autorun is not the same as auto click") and "ไม่ผิดกฎเกมนะครับ" ("it doesn't break game rules") — [LDPlayer TH blog](https://th.ldplayer.net/blog/cookie-run-classic-tips-farm-auto-run-fast-exp.html) (Thai)
- The same post covers the Lab event (ห้องวิจัยน้ำยาแห่งประสบการณ์ลึกลับ, "mysterious EXP potion lab"). It says you can "รับ EXP ได้วันละ 3 ครั้ง" ("claim EXP 3 times a day") and get an EXP x2 buff for long runs — [LDPlayer TH blog](https://th.ldplayer.net/blog/cookie-run-classic-tips-farm-auto-run-fast-exp.html) (Thai)
- The khomkhom.com guide "วิธีปั๊มเลเวล Cookie Run Classic 1-50 ไวสุด" (fastest leveling 1-50; last updated 26/08/2026) gives this staging:
  - Lv 1-15: run the first stage and focus on Bonus Time.
  - Lv 16-30: equip EXP treasures and run Episodes 1-2.
  - Lv 31-50: use an endurance cookie (Pistachio) plus the Firebird pet (นกเพลิง) on deeper stages.
  - It says **Episodes 1-2** have dense jellies and EXP orbs with simple obstacles.
  - Its headline promises results "ใน 1 สัปดาห์" ("in 1 week"), but the body contains no calculation behind that.
  - The page mainly promotes a friend-invite service (9-49 THB), so treat it as marketing — [khomkhom.com](https://khomkhom.com/th/cookierunclassic/guide/cookie-run-classic-fast-leveling-guide) (Thai)
- The CookieGame bot blog (2026-08-31) gives manual tips: run as far as possible, use Relay revives, and grab XP stars ("เก็บได้คือกำไรล้วนๆ", "anything you collect is pure profit"). It also says "EXP มาจากจำนวนรอบสะสม ไม่มีทางลัด" ("EXP comes from total runs, there is no shortcut") — [cookiegamebot.com](https://www.cookiegamebot.com/blog/farm-exp-cookie-run) (Thai, bot seller)
- NNAN Shop (2026-09-05) gives only generic advice: pick a lineup that clears reliably, log time per run and EXP per run, and judge stages by total reward, not EXP alone — [botcookierun.com](https://www.botcookierun.com/blog/cookie-run-classic-exp-leveling-guide) (Thai, bot seller)
- The YouTube video by ZMOS GAMING (2026-07-03, about 123k views) is titled "แนะนำเซ็ตฟาร์ม Auto Run ยอดนิยม เงิน เลเวล กล่อง สายฟรีทำได้ ไม่เสี่ยงโดนแบน" ("popular Auto Run farm sets for money, level and boxes; free-to-play friendly; no ban risk"). Its chapter list includes "14:01 Auto Run XP เลเวล" and "18:23 ทำไมเราต้องฟาร์มเลเวล" ("why we need to farm levels"). The description says it uses "ไม่ใช้มาโคร ไม่ใช้ออโต้คลิก" ("no macros, no auto-click"). It links Korean Auto Run clips that make "ตาละ 100k" ("100k per run") — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o5t0HKGZIO8) (Thai; video content not verified)

### Inferences
- In Thai usage, "Auto Run" means a set that survives with no input, not a macro. Thai players treat it as the legitimate AFK route. Running that loop many times a day on several emulator instances (LDPlayer advises up to 4+ windows) is the most likely legitimate driver of fast leveling.
- The Episode 1 "quit at stage 2" loop is the main short-run trick. It optimizes EXP per minute, not EXP per run.

### Gaps
- No Thai first-hand report of days or hours to reach 110 was found, from Pantip, Facebook, TikTok or YouTube text. TikTok "ฟาร์มเลเวล" discover pages would not render.
- No official Devsisters Thai patch notes on EXP were found.

## 2. Best cookie/pet/treasure combo and map for XP; concrete XP-per-run numbers?

### Takeaway
Thai sources name three cookie/pet/treasure ideas and give three XP figures that contradict each other.

The three set ideas:
- **Cloud Cookie (คุกกี้รสเมฆ) + Cloud Candy + a cloud/Rainbow pet** for constant Bonus Time (LDPlayer).
- **Bright Cookie (คุกกี้ขิง ญ)** for its +EXP% skill.
- The **Carousel (ม้าหมุน, +30% EXP)** and **Melon Guitar (กีตาร์เมล่อน, +20% EXP)** treasures (khomkhom).

The three XP-per-run figures:
- "about 1,000 per short run" (LDPlayer).
- "41,000++" in a July 2026 YouTube title.
- A later video title mentions an **EXP nerf after an update**.

### Cited Findings
- **Cloud Cookie + Cloud Candy** to keep entering Bonus Time, with a cloud pet; the text also names "Rainbows End". **Bright Cookie (คุกกี้ขิง ญ)** for +EXP%. Prioritize EXP-% treasures — [LDPlayer TH blog](https://th.ldplayer.net/blog/cookie-run-classic-tips-farm-auto-run-fast-exp.html) (Thai)
- XP per run: "ได้ทีละเกือบ 1000" ("almost 1,000 each time") for the short Episode 1 loop, and "ถ้าคูน 2 เท่าก็ฉ่ำมาก ๆ" ("with x2 it's really juicy") — [LDPlayer TH blog](https://th.ldplayer.net/blog/cookie-run-classic-tips-farm-auto-run-fast-exp.html) (Thai). No baseline or screenshot is cited.
- Treasures: "ม้าหมุนหรรษา (Carousel): EXP +30%", "กีตาร์เมล่อน (Melon Guitar): EXP +20%", and a Music Box that lengthens Bonus Time. The cookie is Pistachio and the pet is Firebird (นกเพลิง, Legend). The page's FAQ says two EXP treasures make leveling "เร็วขึ้นกว่าเท่าตัว" ("more than twice as fast"), but +30% and +20% only add up to +50% — [khomkhom.com](https://khomkhom.com/th/cookierunclassic/guide/cookie-run-classic-fast-leveling-guide) (Thai)
- The khomkhom page says the Firebird pet is unlocked by inviting **29 friends**, which "ใช้เวลาเกือบ 10 ชั่วโมง" ("takes almost 10 hours"); the site sells this as a service. item2you lists "ชวนเพื่อน 29 คน ราคา 70 บาท" ("invite 29 friends, 70 THB") — [khomkhom.com](https://khomkhom.com/th/cookierunclassic/guide/cookie-run-classic-fast-leveling-guide); item2you only via search snippet, because the page returned HTTP 521 — [item2you](https://item2you.com/services/game?slug=cookierun-classic) (Thai)
- YouTube, MOBYDICK channel (2026-07-29, about 40k views): "3 เซตฟาร์ม EXP ดีที่สุด ทำได้ 41,000++ | CookieRun Classic" ("3 best EXP farming sets, 41,000+"). The description is only sponsor links, so the sets and the exact meaning of 41,000 (per run? per hour?) are unverified — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrGN4d9BuG8) (Thai)
- YouTube, BLUE.Gamecasters (2026-08-01, about 17k views): "รวมทีมฟาร์ม EXP ที่ดีที่สุด! พร้อมสรุปเนิร์ฟ EXP หลังอัปเดต" ("best EXP farm teams, plus a summary of the EXP nerf after the update"). The description has only codes and LDPlayer links, so the nerf details are unverified — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lP8el2POp20) (Thai)
- A Thai fan board (cookierunhub, post by "라이언", dated 2026-05-18, 1,237 views) posts an **EXP-per-level table for levels 1-110**. The table is an image, so the values could not be extracted. The post says it is "แปลด้วยเครื่องต้นฉบับ" ("machine-translated from the original"), probably Korean data — [cookierunhub.com/th/board/399](https://www.cookierunhub.com/th/board/399) (Thai). The May 2026 date is before the June global/Thai launch, so the table likely comes from the earlier Korean/Kakao version.
- Sources disagree on the best map. LDPlayer says Episode 1 only (quit at stage 2). khomkhom says Episodes 1-2, then "ด่านลึกๆ" ("deeper stages") for levels 31-50. CookieGame says longer runs give more EXP: "ยิ่งวิ่งไกล ยิ่งได้เยอะ" ("the further you run, the more you get") — sources as above.

### Inferences
- The figures of about 1,000 per short run and 41,000+ (probably a long Auto Run with x2 and EXP treasures) are not necessarily in conflict. Short-loop versus long-run is the main strategic split.
- The early-August "EXP nerf" title suggests the most generous XP set or mechanic of July 2026 was patched. Players who hit 110 in July may have benefited from pre-nerf rates.

### Gaps
- No verified EXP-per-level totals, and no total EXP needed for 110, from Thai sources. The table is an image.
- Which set gives 41,000+, and what the nerf changed, could not be checked without watching the videos.

## 3. Is there a mechanic that makes leveling much faster than expected?

### Takeaway
The candidate accelerators Thai sources mention are:
- Auto Run sets that need no input, so 24/7 AFK play limited only by lives.
- The daily Lab EXP x2.
- EXP-% treasures.
- XP stars and orbs in Bonus Time.
- Multi-instance emulators.
- Pre-nerf July rates.
- Bots and cheat mods (see Q4).

No Thai source describes friend-bonus, league or carried-over-account mechanics for XP. Coupon codes found give **only crystals**, not EXP.

### Cited Findings
- Lab event: EXP 3 times a day plus EXP x2 for long runs — [LDPlayer TH blog](https://th.ldplayer.net/blog/cookie-run-classic-tips-farm-auto-run-fast-exp.html) (Thai)
- Every level-up gives a Level Bonus (score %), and level 50 unlocks the 3rd treasure slot: "เมื่อถึง เลเวล 50 คุณจะปลดล็อก ช่องใส่สมบัติครบ 3 ช่องเต็ม" ("at level 50 you unlock all 3 treasure slots") — [khomkhom.com](https://khomkhom.com/th/cookierunclassic/guide/cookie-run-classic-fast-leveling-guide) (Thai). An English fan wiki, seen only as a search snippet and not fetched, says levels go up to 110 with a 55% total score bonus as of September 2026.
- Codes (Thai pages): COOKIERUNCLASSIC1M (100 crystals), COOKIERUNCLASSICNO1 (100 crystals), AMAZINGKIWICOOK2 (20 crystals), redeemed with your Player ID. No EXP rewards — [24buym](https://www.24buym.com/blog/cookierun-classic-2026) (Thai). The BLUE.Gamecasters description adds COOKIERUNCLASSIC2M (100 crystals) — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lP8el2POp20)
- A related exploit-adjacent event, about coins rather than XP: in mid-July 2026 a Banana Cookie buff pushed coin runs past an old anti-cheat cap, "ล็อกเพดานไว้ 450,000 เหรียญต่อรอบ" ("the cap was locked at 450,000 coins per run"). This caused mass false bans that were later lifted. The article says this was "ไม่ได้เกิดจากผู้เล่นใช้โปรแกรมแฮกเกมแต่อย่างใด" ("not caused by players using hacking programs at all"). EXP is not mentioned — [24buym blog, 2026-07-17](https://www.24buym.com/blog/cookie-run-classic) (Thai); also covered by [indexgame](https://www.indexgame.in.th/en/article/cookie-run-classic-banana-cookie-auto-ban-fix) (403, not fetched)
- YouTube, DaddyKim (2026-07-16): "CookieRun Classic - อัพเดตใหม่โดนแบนเพียบ !!" ("new update, tons of bans!!"). The description has no details — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=waXuyDF6T4Y) (Thai)

### Inferences
- The July Banana anti-cheat episode shows the server enforces per-run thresholds, at least for coins. Abnormal per-run EXP or score could be similarly capped or flagged; this is unconfirmed for EXP.
- A cheat mod with "ปั้มสกอร์ง่าย" ("easy score pumping"), invincibility and speed (Q4) would let a run last indefinitely. If EXP scales with distance or time ("ยิ่งวิ่งไกล ยิ่งได้เยอะ"), that is a plausible explanation for implausibly fast 110s.

### Gaps
- Whether XP scales with score, distance or time is not stated by any Thai source.
- No Thai mention of friend-score bonuses, league rewards or level-up packages tied to EXP.
- No source on Thai players carrying over Kakao or Korean pre-launch accounts.

## 4. Macro/bot software, cheat mods and paid leveling services (prices, turnaround, bans)

### Takeaway
The Thai market is crowded with **daily-rental Cookie Run Classic bots** at about 19-25 THB/day, some with dedicated **EXP farm modes**, cloud-phone hosting, and LDPlayer/MuMu support. There are also **cheat-mod rentals** (speed hack, god mode, score pump) from 69 THB/day, and account and service shops. **No Thai listing explicitly selling "ปั๊มเวล 110" or "ไอดี เวล 110" was found.** The "ปั้มเลเวล" services that do exist actually sell friend invites and hearts. No confirmed bans specifically for bots or macros were found; the documented ban wave was the false-positive Banana coin cap.

### Inferences
- Cheap 24/7 bots with EXP modes, multiplied across cloud phones or emulator instances, plus score/invincibility cheat mods, are the most plausible explanation for unusually fast level-110 accounts in Thailand. Legitimate Auto Run AFK would also be fast, but it is limited by lives and attention.
- Account resellers advertise skipping "a month of farming". This loosely suggests sellers consider about a month of farming normal, but it is not a level-110 figure.

### Gaps
- No Thai listing found advertising a level-110 account or a "รับปั๊มเวล 110" service with price or turnaround. Shopee and Lazada results did not surface; Facebook Marketplace and groups were not indexed.
- No confirmed reports of Classic accounts banned specifically for bots, macros or mods, and no Devsisters Thai statement on automation.
- Prices on the 499K, JOHNNY SHOP and item2you pages were not verifiable (403/521 errors).
