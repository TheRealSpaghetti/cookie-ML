# Korean-language sources: how players reach max level 110 in Cookie Run Classic (쿠키런 클래식)

Research date: 2026-10-12. Sources are Korean (KO) unless marked EN.

**Read this first.** "Korean players of Cookie Run Classic" are *not* playing the global app `com.devsisters.crg`. The global build launched on 2026-06-25 and is **geo-blocked in South Korea**, because Korea already has the original *Cookie Run for Kakao*. The Korean game is the 2013 title (Play package `com.devsisters.CookieRunForKakao`), which is now also branded **쿠키런 클래식** ("formerly 쿠키런 for Kakao"). The two builds share episodes and updates: the 2026-07-23 update shipped in both at once. Korean community material on "만렙 110" therefore comes from the Kakao build, where many accounts are 10+ years old. Korean posts are tagged **[카쿠]** (Kakao Cookie Run) or **[오븐]** (galleries' tag; often used for all Cookie Run posts). Posts tagged [오븐] that are about OvenBreak are flagged below.

**Fetch limitation:** namu.wiki, its mirror namu.moe and alphawiki all returned 403/522 errors, so I could not open them directly. Every namu.wiki claim below comes from search-engine result excerpts of those pages and is marked **(namu, via search excerpt)**. Treat those as likely but unverified. DC Inside, Inven, Byline, Threads, a-ha and cookierun.wiki pages were opened and read.

## Q1. What do Korean players report as the fastest XP route to 110, and how long did it take?

### Takeaway
I found no Korean source that describes a fast or recent climb to 110. Korean high-level accounts come from years of play on the 2013 Kakao game, built up across level-cap raises (cap 100 since August 2016, 110 by at least May 2026). Korean guides describe XP as roughly proportional to *time alive in a run*, multiplied by 경험치 2배 (Double XP) and other multipliers. The routes they name are a time-efficient "Alchemist run" combo on Episode 6, Ice Wave Tower (얼음파도의 탑) floor 54, a daily XP event episode, and one-shot XP potion treasures. No source gives hours or days to reach 110.

### Cited Findings
- **Korea is excluded from the global build.** cookierun.wiki (EN): CookieRun Classic is "not available in a select few regions, including South Korea and India … due to the existence of Cookie Run for Kakao and Cookie Run India"; it is "a globalized English version of Cookie Run for Kakao"; global release 2026-06-25. — [cookierun.wiki (EN)](https://cookierun.wiki/w/Cookie_Run:_Classic)
- **Korean branding and history.** Korean press calls it 「쿠키런 클래식(구 쿠키런 for Kakao)」 ("Cookie Run Classic, formerly Cookie Run for Kakao"). Devsisters says it opened a global server for a game that until then ran only in Korea, and the 7/23 update was the first one released 「국내외 통합」 ("unified domestic + overseas"). — [Byline Network, 2026-06-29 (KO)](https://byline.network/2026/06/29-527/) (summary from search excerpt; page not opened directly)
- **The Korean branding predates the global launch.** An Inven article from 2025-03-28 is titled 「쿠키런 클래식, 12주년 기념 업데이트 진행」 ("Cookie Run Classic holds 12th-anniversary update"). — [Inven, 2025-03-28 (KO)](https://www.inven.co.kr/webzine/news/?news=304411&site=csgo)
- **Level-cap history.** The account max level became 100 with the August 2016 「신대륙 발견」 ("New Continent Discovered") update. — (namu 쿠키런/역사, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런/역사 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%97%AD%EC%82%AC). I found no dated source for the 100→110 raise.
- **110 existed in Korea before the global launch.** A Korean Threads post dated 2026-05-11 reads 「쿠키런 정지 당했다 110 레벨이 코앞이었는데 …. 매크로 걸림」 ("Got banned from Cookie Run, I was right about to hit level 110… caught for macro"). — [Threads @code_199102, 2026-05-11 (KO)](https://www.threads.com/@code_199102/post/DYNl4BDEnwt)
- **Current cap and score bonus.** 「2026년 9월 기준 레벨 110까지 열려 있어 총 55%의 점수보너스를 받을 수 있다」 ("As of Sept 2026, levels up to 110 are open, giving a total 55% score bonus"). Bonus per level is +1% up to level 50 (49% at Lv50), then +0.1% per level from 51 (55% at Lv110). — (namu, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0); [namu.wiki 쿠키런/팁 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%ED%8C%81)
  - A DC guide from 2024-02-13 says the same thing: 「50렙부터 레벨 보너스가 49퍼가 되고, 이후 10레벨 오를때 마다 1퍼씩 늘어난다」 ("From Lv50 the level bonus is 49%, then +1% every 10 levels"). — [DC 쿠키런 마이너 갤러리 #970832, 2024-02-13 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=970832)
- **The level-110 reward.** Reaching Lv.110 automatically grants the treasure 「로얄 쿠키스 클럽 트로피 (S급)」 (Royal Cookies Club Trophy). Its holding effect is a 1–12.5% chance of 12 crystals at attendance. namu warns: 「판매 시 재구매가 불가능하므로, 절대로 이 보물을 팔아서는 안 되고」 ("it cannot be rebought if sold, so never sell it"). — (namu, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런/보물 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EB%B3%B4%EB%AC%BC)
- **Weekly rewards by level.** namu's weekly reward table has a separate 110–119 bracket (row 「110~119 | 4 | 8,000 | 130」; column meanings unclear from the excerpt). This suggests the developers expect levels above 110 in future. — (namu, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0)
- **High levels are slow.** A 2020 DC guide: 「경험치 노가다를 하지 않는 이상 레벨 올리기가 갈수록 힘들어지므로」 ("unless you grind XP, leveling gets harder and harder"). — [DC #295400 "쿠키런 for kakao 가이드 (상)", 2020-08-13 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=295400)
  - namu, about Tower floor 54: 「80레벨 이상의 높은 레벨로 갈수록 경험치가 정말 안 오르므로」 ("at Lv80+ XP really barely rises"). — (namu, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런/추천 조합 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%B6%94%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A1%B0%ED%95%A9)
- **Time scale.** The only time figure I found comes from a 2024 newbie guide, whose author says it took about one year to reach the mid-game scoring stage. It also recommends adding friends 「하루에 10~20명씩」 ("10–20 per day") toward 333 friends. — [DC #1053696 "카카오 쿠키런 뉴비들을 위한 길라잡이", 2024-06-08 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=1053696)

### Inferences
- If a non-Korean player sees "Korean players" at 110 on a leaderboard, the likeliest explanation is account age, not a secret fast route. Most Korean accounts with 110 probably carried a level near 100 over from 2016–2025 and only needed the last 10 levels after the cap rose. (This is my inference; no source states it outright.) The global build is new as of 2026-06, so global players start from level 1.
- XP is time-based and the level bonus is tiny above 50 (+0.1% per level). That explains why Korean guides describe Lv80+ as a slog.

### Gaps
- I found no Korean post giving the hours, days or weeks a player spent going from a low level to 110, nor the date 110 was introduced.
- I found no XP-required-per-level table (namu was inaccessible).

## Q2. Which cookie/pet/treasure combo and which map or mode is considered best for XP? Concrete XP-per-run numbers?

### Takeaway
Korean guides (namu 추천 조합; DC gallery) name four XP sources: an "Alchemist run" (연금술사런) combo on Episode 6, a special-episode setup, a limited daily XP event episode, and Ice Wave Tower floor 54. The headline figure is "high 30k to mid 50k XP in about 1 minute" on Episode 6. All of these figures come from namu excerpts I could not open directly.

### Cited Findings
- **XP formula (namu).** 「경험치는 플레이 시간에 비례하여 얻어지며 1초당 10경험치가 산정된다」 ("XP is proportional to play time, 10 XP per second"). 「경험치 2배는 필수이며, 오래 달려야 하기에 체력 강화와 체력감소 15% 느림 부스트를 사용하는 것이 좋다」 ("Double XP is mandatory; since you need to run long, use the energy upgrade and the 15%-slower-energy-drain boost"). — (namu, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런/추천 조합 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%B6%94%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A1%B0%ED%95%A9)
- **Cap on multipliers in regular episodes.** 「스페셜 에피소드를 제외한 일반 에피소드의 경험치 배율은 최대 5배까지가 한계이다」 ("in regular (non-special) episodes, the XP multiplier caps at 5x"), so players should avoid stacking wasted multipliers. — (namu, via search excerpt) same page.
- **Best XP-per-minute combo: the Alchemist run on Episode 6.** 「에피소드 6에서 달리며 … 3만 후반~5만 중반의 경험치를 벌 수 있는데 반해 플레이타임은 1분정도밖에 안걸리는 최고효율 조합」 ("run in Episode 6 … earns high-30k to mid-50k XP while taking only about 1 minute: the most efficient combo"). The yield depends on holding-effect procs. — (namu, via search excerpt) same page.
- **"XP party" holding effect.** Collect 「카이막&금강법사&연근요괴&동파육맛 쿠키」 (Kaymak, Vajra Master, Lotus-root Yokai and Dongpo-pork cookies) to unlock an 「경험치 파티 보유효과」 ("XP party" collection or holding bonus). — (namu, via search excerpt) same page. These are recent cookies; I could not determine whether they are in the global build under English names.
- **Special episode route.** 「2분 못 되는 시간에 3미박과 바다요정의 마음 풀강 기준 5,000경험치」 ("under 2 minutes: 5,000 XP with 3 mystery boxes and fully upgraded 'Sea Fairy's Heart'"). — (namu, via search excerpt) same page / [namu.wiki 쿠키런/보물/스페셜 에피소드 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EB%B3%B4%EB%AC%BC/%EC%8A%A4%ED%8E%98%EC%85%9C%20%EC%97%90%ED%94%BC%EC%86%8C%EB%93%9C)
- **Event episode 「경험의 물약 마법공방」 ("Potion of Experience Magic Workshop").** It is an XP-only event episode with 「일일 플레이 횟수 3회 제한」 ("limited to 3 plays per day"). Treasure abilities do not apply, and a perfect run gives about 「17,000 ~ 18,000경험치」. — (namu, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런/추천 조합 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%B6%94%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A1%B0%ED%95%A9)
- **Ice Wave Tower floor 54.** 「얼음파도의 탑 54층에서 경험치 노가다가 가능하다. 신규 혹은 복귀 경험치 2배 기간에 여기를 빡세게 돌리면 경험치를 대량 얻을 수 있다」 ("You can grind XP on Ice Wave Tower floor 54. Run it hard during the new-player or returning-player Double-XP period for large XP"). — [DC #970832, 2024-02-13 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=970832). namu adds that this falls off at Lv80+ (see Q1).
- **XP treasures.** The DC guide calls popsicle, hardcover book and lip balm treasures 「경험치 보물」 (XP treasures), 「초반 경험치 작이나 후반 100렙 달리기 등 여러 곳에서 유용」 ("useful early, and later for the run to Lv100"). — [DC #970832 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=970832)
- **XP cookies.** 명랑한 쿠키 (Cheerful Cookie, unlocked at 30,000 points) is "essential" for XP grinding, and 탐험가맛 쿠키 (Explorer Cookie, unlocked at 200 friends) is 「경험치 노가다에도 쓰인다」 ("also used for XP grinding"). — [DC #295400, 2020-08-13 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=295400)
- **Early-level advice.** 「레벨 50 이전까지는 점수 너무 연연하지 말자」 ("before Lv50, don't obsess over score"). — [DC #970832 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=970832)
- **Unreliable source.** An a-ha.io Q&A from 2025-10-12 ("Lv27 → 50 fast?") mixes in mechanics from other games (guilds, healers, "XP jelly booster"). It looks generic or AI-written and should not be relied on. — [a-ha.io (KO)](https://www.a-ha.io/questions/44ec7a28ed3f853d9d3dc71a56622f9f)

### Inferences
- At 10 XP per second, Double XP and a cap of 5x on regular episodes, a 1-minute run would give about 600 base XP, or about 3,000 XP at 5x. The "30k–55k in 1 minute" Alchemist figure must therefore come from cookie, treasure or holding-effect XP sources that add flat XP or bypass the base formula, such as Alchemist conversions or bonus procs. The excerpts do not explain this; verify in-game.
- For a bot, the combination these sources describe as most time-efficient is Episode 6 with the Alchemist combo plus Double XP. A daily 3-run event episode would add to that when available.

### Gaps
- I could not read the exact Alchemist-run cookie, pet and treasure list, because the namu page returned 403.
- I found no Korean source that gives cookie or pet names in English, or that maps these combos to the global build.

## Q3. Is there an XP mechanic that makes leveling much faster than expected?

### Takeaway
Yes, several. The biggest factor for Korean players is **account carry-over**: their accounts date from the 2013 Kakao game, while the global build starts fresh. Within the game itself, the accelerators are Double XP (described as mandatory and kept on until 100), **new-player and returning-player Double-XP periods**, one-shot XP potion treasures, the daily XP event episode, and collection or holding XP bonuses. XP is based on survival time, not score.

### Cited Findings
- **Account carry-over.** The Korean game is the same title as Cookie Run for Kakao from 2013, with continuous accounts. The global build is a separate, region-locked release. — [cookierun.wiki (EN)](https://cookierun.wiki/w/Cookie_Run:_Classic); [Byline 2026-06-29 (KO)](https://byline.network/2026/06/29-527/)
- **Double XP is kept on continuously.** Above Lv50, required XP balloons without Double XP, so most high-level players stock up on 경험치 2배 boosts: 「돈이 매우 부족한 극초반을 지나고 나면 100렙이 될 때까지 계속 켜두자」 ("once past the cash-starved very early game, keep it on until Lv100"). — (namu 쿠키런/게임 요소, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EA%B2%8C%EC%9E%84%20%EC%9A%94%EC%86%8C)
- **New-player and returning-player Double XP periods.** 「신규 혹은 복귀 경험치 2배 기간」 ("new or returning Double-XP period"). — [DC #970832 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=970832). namu's tips page also says to use a Double-XP event to reach Lv30 and unlock 천사맛 쿠키 (Angel Cookie). — (via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런/팁 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%ED%8C%81)
- **XP potion treasures 「경험의 영약」.** These are one-shot treasures that grant a fixed amount of XP. namu says they suit 「빨리 레벨을 올리고 싶은 유저」 ("users who want to level fast") but are weak at high levels because the amount is fixed. — (namu, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런/보물 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EB%B3%B4%EB%AC%BC). They can be bought with league medals in the medal shop. — [DC #295400 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=295400)
- **Equip-type XP bonus treasures** become useless at max level. — (namu, via search excerpt) [namu.wiki 쿠키런/보물 (KO)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EB%B3%B4%EB%AC%BC)
- **Events I found were not XP events.** The 12th-anniversary event (2025-03-28) gave every login 「크리스탈 1,200개, 코인 12만개」 (1,200 crystals and 120k coins) plus a title-frame treasure. No XP event was mentioned. — [Inven 2025-03-28 (KO)](https://www.inven.co.kr/webzine/news/?news=304411&site=csgo). The 2026-01-16 update added Episode 6 「황천의 매화궁」 and new cookies, with no level or XP changes reported. — [Byline 2026-01-16 (KO)](https://byline.network/2026/01/16-605/)

### Inferences
- XP depends on survival time and multipliers, not score, so a bot's best XP per hour depends on how long each run lasts. Being able to survive with energy upgrades and slower energy drain matters, but multipliers above 5x are wasted on regular episodes.
- Global players may get the "new player Double XP" period. A bot should check for it and make the most of it.

### Gaps
- I found no Korean evidence of level-up potions sold for cash, "level-up packages" (레벨업 패키지), coupons that grant XP, or friend or leaderboard XP bonuses.
- I could not confirm whether the global build has the same new or returning Double-XP period.

## Q4. What macro/automation software do they discuss, and are there ban reports?

### Takeaway
DC Inside's Cookie Run gallery has long-standing macro guides. They cover emulator key-recorders (Nox, MuMu, BlueStacks), AutoHotKey image-search scripts and phone tools (FRep, Automatic Clicker, Samsung Game Tuner). The one concrete anti-detection tip is to randomize click positions within regions. There is at least one first-person report (May 2026) of a Kakao-version account banned for macro use just before Lv110.

### Cited Findings
- **2018 macro guide** (tagged [오븐]; the stage names suggest **OvenBreak**, not Kakao, so treat the specifics as OvenBreak-era):
  - It covers PC emulator key recording on Nox and MuMu, which it calls easy but fragile on delays, and AutoHotKey with image search, which handles more but needs coding.
  - On phones it covers Samsung Game Tuner (feature since removed), Automatic Clicker, and **FRep**, which it calls the strongest phone option (ADB / USB-debugging setup via `win.bat`).
  - Core idea: 「시작 – 확인 – 재시작만 반복 하는 행동을 자동으로 실행하는 것」 ("automating the repeated start → confirm → restart loop"). Advice: 「충분한 딜레이를 주는 것이 필요하다」 ("you need to give enough delay").
  - It does not discuss ban risk. 65k views.
  - — [DC #50085 "쿠키런 매크로 가이드", 2018-09-24 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=50085)
- **2022 Kakao-version (카쿠) macro tip:**
  - It uses BlueStacks with image-matched clicks on 게임시작 → 게임시작 → 확인 → 모두열기 → 확인 (Start → Start → OK → Open All → OK).
  - The author found an anti-bot mini-game kept appearing. Their fix was clicking random coordinates inside each matched region: 「같은 위치에 반복된 클릭이 문제였던거임」 ("repeated clicks at the same position were the problem"). Goal: 「미니게임을 아예 안뜨게 하면된다」 ("make the mini-game never appear").
  - The author admits this is a guess. 32k views.
  - — [DC #682065 "카쿠 매크로 궁금해 하던 갤러 있던데 팁 준다", 2022-04-13 (KO)](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=682065)
- **Another macro how-to thread** (not opened): [DC #231872 "매크로 쓰는법 정리해준다" (KO)](https://m.dcinside.com/board/cookierun/231872)
- **Ban question thread.** It asks whether anyone was banned for macros and gets no confirmed answer. — [DC #52575 "매크로 정지 당한사람잇냐?" (KO)](https://m.dcinside.com/board/cookierun/52575?boardtype=I) (search excerpt only). Similar question on a different community: [tdgall "쿠키런 매크로 쓰면 정지 먹어?" (KO)](https://tdgall.com/80396677) (not opened).
- **Ban report, 2026.** 「ㅅㅂ 쿠키런 정지 당했다 110 레벨이 코앞이었는데 …. 매크로 걸림 ;; 그동안 즐거웠다 쿠키런 카카오 . 이제 삭제」 ("F—, got banned from Cookie Run, level 110 was right in front of me… caught macroing. Cookie Run Kakao was fun. Deleting now."). No replies. — [Threads @code_199102, 2026-05-11 (KO)](https://www.threads.com/@code_199102/post/DYNl4BDEnwt)
- **BlueStacks markets its macro recorder** for Cookie Run titles, though the Korean blog post is about Kingdom: 「매일 챙겨줘야 하는 반복적인 작업, 블루스택5의 매크로에게 맡기세요!」 ("Leave the daily repetitive tasks to BlueStacks 5's macro"). — [BlueStacks KO blog (KO)](https://www.bluestacks.com/ko/blog/bluestacks-exclusives/cookie-run-kingdom-eco-mode-ko.html)

### Inferences
- The Kakao game has some anti-automation measures. Users report a mini-game check that appears, apparently triggered by repetitive same-coordinate input, and at least one ban in 2026 for macro use while grinding to 110. Grinding to 110 is therefore a known macro target that Devsisters enforces against. The global build likely shares this anti-cheat, since it shares the codebase and updates.
- Korean macro use described in these sources is mostly for **coins, mystery boxes and gacha items** (start-confirm-restart loops). I found no Korean post about a macro built specifically for XP.

### Gaps
- I found no Korean discussion of Auto.js, LDPlayer-specific scripts, or paid bot services for this game.
- I found no official Devsisters statement on macro sanctions for Cookie Run Classic, and no data on how common bans are.

## Q5. Are there leaderboard/ranking sites showing level distributions?

### Takeaway
