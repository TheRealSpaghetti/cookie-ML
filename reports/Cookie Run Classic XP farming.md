# High-yield runs, not years, explain level 110

Reaching level 110 in Cookie Run Classic does not take years. It takes roughly **3,000 runs if a run yields about 40k XP, or 8,000 to 30,000 runs at ordinary coin-farming yields**, and players close that gap by combining XP per run with automation. The only level curve found puts the climb from 1 to 110 at about **125 million XP** ([cookierundb.com, EN](https://cookierundb.com/economy/levels)). Korean wiki excerpts describe an "Alchemist run" on **Episode 6 that earns high-30k to mid-50k XP in about one minute** ([namu.wiki, KO, via search excerpt](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%B6%94%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A1%B0%ED%95%A9)). A Thai video title claims "41,000++" XP sets ([MOBYDICK on YouTube, TH](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrGN4d9BuG8)). At around 45k XP per run, 110 needs about **26 runs a day for 109 days**, which an emulator bot can do easily. Thai bot vendors sell exactly this as an "EXP mode": short runs on Episode 6 that exit early ([cookiegamebot.com, TH/EN](https://www.cookiegamebot.com/)), or 12-second rounds ([jlbotcookierun.me, TH](https://jlbotcookierun.me/)). A whole industry farms lives (hearts) to feed those runs. Part of the "thousands at 110" may also be a different group: Korean players on the 2013 Kakao build, whose accounts carried levels for a decade. No source in any language reports how long anyone actually took to reach 110, so treat the timeline as arithmetic, not testimony. For your bot, the main gap is not coins (about 150k per run already matches the best open-source bot). It is that **nobody, including you, has measured Classic's XP per run**. The first step is to log XP per run and then test short versus long runs on Episode 6.

## 125 million XP shrinks to weeks once per-run XP is high

The arithmetic works only if the level curve is right, and that curve has a weak pedigree. cookierundb.com lists XP-to-next-level for all 110 levels with no datamine attribution. Its figures include 1,285,900 XP at level 100, then **4.2M to 5.3M XP per level for levels 105 to 110**. Summed, that is **89.1M XP to level 100 and about 125.04M to level 110**, so the last ten levels cost about 36M XP on their own ([cookierundb.com, EN](https://cookierundb.com/economy/levels)). The table has oddities: level 7 costs less than level 6, and the cost jumps 3.3x between levels 100 and 105. A Thai fan board posted a 1-to-110 EXP table as an image, labelled machine-translated from the original and dated May 2026, before the global launch. That suggests the published curve comes from the Korean Kakao build ([cookierunhub.com board 399, TH](https://www.cookierunhub.com/th/board/399)). The 125M figure is therefore the best available number, not a verified one.

The table below divides 125M XP by every per-run XP figure the research found, over the 109 days from the global launch on June 25, 2026 ([Wikipedia, EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cookie_Run_(video_game))) to today. The point is how much the answer depends on the per-run figure.

The table answers the "seems to require years" puzzle. If per-run XP were stuck near the 4k that a generic coin bot's sample panel shows, 110 would take about 277 runs and lives a day, which is unrealistic without industrial heart farming. If a run yields 40k or more, 110 needs fewer than 30 one-minute runs a day, and an AFK set or bot clears that in well under an hour of play. The second regime is the plausible mainstream explanation.

The mechanics behind those yields are only partly understood. Korean wiki excerpts describe the original formula as **10 XP per second survived**, with Double XP "mandatory" and a **5x XP multiplier cap on regular (non-special) episodes** ([namu.wiki, KO, search excerpt](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%B6%94%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A1%B0%ED%95%A9)). The old English Fandom XP page also says 10 base XP per second, but it documents the LINE/Kakao era ([Fandom XP Farming, EN](https://cookierun.fandom.com/wiki/XP_Farming)). Under that formula a one-minute run makes at most about 3,000 XP. The 30k–55k Alchemist figure must therefore come from something outside the time-based formula. The excerpt says the yield depends on holding-effect procs, so it likely comes from flat or proc-based XP. This is my inference, and it matters for your bot. If a large share of XP is flat per run, short runs beat long runs on both XP per minute and XP per life. The evidence also conflicts on what XP tracks. Korean sources say survival time, an English SEO wiki says distance and combo score ([cookierunclassic.online, EN](https://cookierunclassic.online/farming/cookie-run-classic-farming-guide)), and the khm0612 bot author measured that the x2 Point Booster "doubles XP/score, not coins" ([khm0612 PROJECT.md, EN](https://github.com/khm0612/cookierun-classic/blob/main/docs/PROJECT.md)).

## Korean "110s" mostly come from a different, decade-old build

The Korean community is not playing com.devsisters.crg. The global build is unavailable in South Korea and India because Korea already has Cookie Run for Kakao. Classic is described as "a globalized English version" of that 2013 game, with DevPlay login instead of KakaoTalk ([cookierun.wiki, EN](https://cookierun.wiki/w/CookieRun:_Classic)). Korean press calls the Kakao game "쿠키런 클래식(구 쿠키런 for Kakao)" ("Cookie Run Classic, formerly Cookie Run for Kakao"). The July 23, 2026 update was the first one released to both builds at once ([Byline Network, KO, search excerpt](https://byline.network/2026/06/29-527/)). The Korean branding predates the global launch: Inven used it for the 12th-anniversary update in March 2025 ([Inven, KO](https://www.inven.co.kr/webzine/news/?news=304411&site=csgo)). Korean accounts have been continuous since 2013, and the level cap rose to 100 in August 2016 ([namu.wiki history, KO, search excerpt](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%97%AD%EC%82%AC)). Level 110 already existed there before Classic launched globally. A May 11, 2026 Korean Threads post complains of being banned for macro use while level 110 was "right in front of me" ([Threads @code_199102, KO](https://www.threads.com/@code_199102/post/DYNl4BDEnwt)). Korean wiki excerpts also list a weekly-reward bracket for levels 110–119 ([namu.wiki, KO, search excerpt](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0)).

So if the max-level players you see include Korean accounts, many probably carried a level near 100 for years and needed only the last ten levels. This is my inference; no source states it directly. Whether Kakao accounts even appear on global leaderboards is unresolved. Devsisters calls Classic a separate global server ([Byline Network, KO](https://byline.network/2026/06/29-527/)), but store copy promises leagues "with players worldwide" ([BlueStacks KO campaign page, KO](https://www.bluestacks.com/campaign/com.devsisters.crg/ko/)). Every account on the global build itself started at level 1 after June 25, because no account transfer from Kakao is documented ([cookierun.wiki, EN](https://cookierun.wiki/w/CookieRun:_Classic)). Global 110s therefore have to be explained by the per-run yields and automation in the other sections. No source found, in Korean, Thai or English, gives a player's real time from level 1 to 110. The closest is a 2024 Korean newbie guide whose author took about a year to reach the mid-game ([DC Inside #1053696, KO](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=1053696)). No public site shows how account levels are distributed, so "thousands at 110" is your own observation and cannot be checked against data.

## Episode 6, XP treasures and the daily potion lab carry the legitimate route

Thai and Korean sources independently point to the same map. The Korean wiki excerpt calls the Alchemist run (연금술사런) on **Episode 6** "the most efficient combo" for XP per minute ([namu.wiki, KO, search excerpt](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%B6%94%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A1%B0%ED%95%A9)). The Thai bot CookieGame builds its paid "EXP mode" around farming **Episode 6 and exiting when the round ends** ([cookiegamebot.com, TH/EN](https://www.cookiegamebot.com/)). Episode 6 reached the Kakao build in January 2026 ([Byline Network, KO](https://byline.network/2026/01/16-605/)). The same excerpt mentions an "XP party" holding effect (경험치 파티 보유효과) unlocked by collecting the Kaymak, Vajra Master (금강법사), Lotus-root Yokai (연근요괴) and Dongpo-pork cookies. Lotus Root Phantom Cookie and the Lotus Sitter pet arrived in the global build on June 25, 2026 ([@CRC_news on X, EN](https://x.com/CRC_news/status/2070075773545844955)), and the same tier list ranks Lotus Root Phantom as the top cookie ([cookierunclassicwiki.site, EN](https://cookierunclassicwiki.site/tier-list/)). That fits the idea that 연근요괴 is the same cookie under its English name, but it is my inference. The exact Alchemist-run cookie, pet and treasure lineup could not be read because namu.wiki returned 403 errors.

Both language communities describe the same daily XP event. The Thai LDPlayer blog describes a "mysterious EXP potion lab" (ห้องวิจัยน้ำยาแห่งประสบการณ์ลึกลับ) where you can claim EXP three times a day and get an EXP x2 buff for long runs ([LDPlayer TH blog, TH](https://th.ldplayer.net/blog/cookie-run-classic-tips-farm-auto-run-fast-exp.html)). Korean excerpts describe "경험의 물약 마법공방" (Potion of Experience Magic Workshop), an XP-only episode limited to three plays a day, where treasures do not apply and a perfect run gives **17,000–18,000 XP** ([namu.wiki, KO, search excerpt](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%B6%94%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A1%B0%ED%95%A9)). At that rate, three perfect runs a day over 109 days give at most about 5.7M XP, under 5% of the total. It is worth automating, but it does not explain mass 110s.

Thai sources name specific gear:

- **Treasures:** Carousel (ม้าหมุน, +30% EXP), Melon Guitar (กีตาร์เมล่อน, +20% EXP), and a Music Box that extends Bonus Time ([khomkhom.com, TH](https://khomkhom.com/th/cookierunclassic/guide/cookie-run-classic-fast-leveling-guide)).
- **Cookies:** Bright Cookie for +EXP%, and Cloud Cookie with Cloud Candy and a cloud or Rainbow pet for repeated Bonus Time on Auto Run ([LDPlayer TH blog, TH](https://th.ldplayer.net/blog/cookie-run-classic-tips-farm-auto-run-fast-exp.html)).
- **Endurance set for deeper stages at levels 31 to 50:** Pistachio Cookie with the Firebird pet (นกเพลิง). The Firebird is unlocked by inviting 29 friends, and the same site sells invites as a service ([khomkhom.com, TH](https://khomkhom.com/th/cookierunclassic/guide/cookie-run-classic-fast-leveling-guide)).

Older Korean guides add XP treasures (popsicle, hardcover book, lip balm) and Ice Wave Tower floor 54 as a Double-XP grind. Both are Kakao-era advice, and namu says XP "really barely rises" at level 80 and above ([DC Inside #970832, KO](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=970832); [namu.wiki, KO, search excerpt](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%BF%A0%ED%82%A4%EB%9F%B0/%EC%B6%94%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A1%B0%ED%95%A9)). Korean excerpts also recommend using new-player and returning-player Double-XP periods hard ([DC Inside #970832, KO](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=970832)). The global store promises unspecified "buffs just for new players" ([BlueStacks listing, EN](https://www.bluestacks.com/campaign/com.devsisters.crg/ms/)).

Thai sources disagree on run length. LDPlayer recommends looping Episode 1 and quitting at stage 2, about two minutes per game and "almost 1,000" XP each ([LDPlayer TH blog, TH](https://th.ldplayer.net/blog/cookie-run-classic-tips-farm-auto-run-fast-exp.html)). CookieGame's own blog says "the further you run, the more you get" and that EXP "comes from total runs, there is no shortcut" ([cookiegamebot.com blog, TH](https://www.cookiegamebot.com/blog/farm-exp-cookie-run)). Timing matters too. An August 1 Thai video is titled as a summary of the "EXP nerf after the update" ([BLUE.Gamecasters, TH](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lP8el2POp20)). In Thai usage, "Auto Run" means a cookie set that survives with no input, not a macro. Thai creators present it as the safe AFK route. A 123k-view video sells "Auto Run" money, level and box sets as free-to-play and without ban risk, and says it uses no macros or auto-click ([ZMOS GAMING, TH](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o5t0HKGZIO8)). Multi-instance Auto Run is the most likely large-scale legitimate-looking route.

## Cheap Thai bots, heart farms and cheat mods do the rest

The software market is mostly Thai, cheap, and openly built for XP. Bots rent for about ฿15–35 a day:

- **CookieGame Pro** adds the Episode 6 EXP mode and reports XP per round to Discord ([cookiegamebot.com, TH/EN](https://www.cookiegamebot.com/)). Its blog promises "leave it overnight, wake up to higher levels" ([cookiegamebot.com blog, TH](https://www.cookiegamebot.com/blog/farm-exp-cookie-run)).
- **JL Bot** sells an "EXP Only (very fast)" mode at about **12 seconds per round**. It quotes per-round caps of 39,999 EXP and 9,999 coins, versus 99,999 coins and the same 39,999 EXP on its 150-second Episode 1 mode ([jlbotcookierun.me, TH](https://jlbotcookierun.me/)).
- **CookieRunBot** offers a "stop jumping after N jumps" option, unlimited screens on its Max plan, and rented cloud phones and cloud emulators ([cookierunbot.com, TH](https://www.cookierunbot.com/)).

Two independent vendors selling short-round XP modes is the strongest available evidence that XP per run does not scale linearly with run length. That is an inference from product design, not a measurement. JL Bot's identical 39,999 EXP cap on a 12-second mode and a 150-second mode is odd. It fits either a flat per-run XP component or a self-imposed ceiling under a suspected server check. Its "Login Failed bypass" and its requirement for the user's Gmail and password suggest some modes may run server-side rather than as screen automation ([jlbotcookierun.me, TH](https://jlbotcookierun.me/)). That is speculative but would carry far higher risk.

Lives are the hard limit for everyone, and a supporting industry has grown around them:

- **Alt-account farming:** JL Bot sells hearts at about ฿0.30 each, runs invite-link heart farming, and sells login bypasses in batches of 1,000–10,000 IDs ([jlbotcookierun.me, TH](https://jlbotcookierun.me/)).
- **Heart pumping by account ID:** CookieRunBot offers "heart pumping via MID" ([cookierunbot.com, TH](https://www.cookierunbot.com/)).
- **Pay-per-unit services:** item2you's "level pumping" page actually sells friend invites (29 for ฿70) and hearts (2 for ฿1), seen only as a search snippet ([item2you, TH](https://item2you.com/services/game?slug=cookierun-classic)).
- **Old-style friend lists:** Korean veterans built 333-friend lists by adding 10–20 friends a day ([DC Inside #1053696, KO](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=1053696)).

Above that sit cheat mods. TAVISSHOP rents Android builds with Speed Hack, Invincible and "easy score pumping" from ฿69 a day. It advises not leaving features on all day or posting abnormal leaderboard scores ([tavisshop.com, TH](https://tavisshop.com/cookierunclassic/)). An invincible run never ends, so if XP tracks survival time this is a plausible route to absurd XP. I found no source confirming anyone used it for levels. No Thai listing was found selling a level-110 account or a "ปั๊มเวล 110" (level-110 boosting) service.

The open-source side is smaller:

- **max180643/cookierun-classic-bot** (your upstream) drives menus only.
- **khm0612/cookierun-classic** runs a learned 60 Hz jump/slide policy that averages about 1.86 pit falls and about 336-second runs, and enforces `forbid_crystals: true` ([khm0612, EN](https://github.com/khm0612/cookierun-classic); [PROJECT.md, EN](https://github.com/khm0612/cookierun-classic/blob/main/docs/PROJECT.md)).
- **balagux/cookierun-classic-bot** OCRs Coins and EXP on the result screen, sends hearts, claims lives from the mailbox, and solves the anti-bot card check by reading "jumping" or "sliding" from the header ([balagux, TH/EN](https://github.com/balagux/cookierun-classic-bot); [release v1.4.21, EN](https://github.com/balagux/cookierun-classic-bot/releases/tag/v1.4.21)).

Ban risk is real and documented, but not quantified. Around early July 2026, Devsisters ran a "CookieRun Classic Fair Play Campaign" warning that listed activities "may result in a permanent account suspension". The list itself could not be retrieved ([@CRC_news on X, EN](https://x.com/CRC_news/status/2074502714608759225)). The precedent is concrete: Devsisters permanently suspended 430 OvenBreak users in 2024 for "illegal programs, application tampering, macros" ([Fandom patch notes, EN](https://cookierun.fandom.com/wiki/Patch_Notes/CROB/April_16,_2024_(2))). In mid-July 2026, a Banana Cookie buff pushed coin runs past a server cap "locked at 450,000 coins per run", triggering mass false bans that were later lifted ([24buym, TH](https://www.24buym.com/blog/cookie-run-classic)). That episode proves the server flags abnormal per-run totals, at least for coins. An XP-per-run threshold is plausible but unconfirmed. Korean macro users report that repeated clicks at the same coordinates trigger the anti-bot mini-game ([DC Inside #682065, KO](https://gall.dcinside.com/mgallery/board/view/?id=cookierun&no=682065)), and one Kakao player reports a macro ban just short of 110 ([Threads, KO](https://www.threads.com/@code_199102/post/DYNl4BDEnwt)). Roughly from highest to lowest risk: account sharing with sellers, alt-account heart farms with IP switching, cheat mods and possible server-side modes, then local ADB screen bots like yours. Yours is the lowest-risk tier, but it is still against the Terms of Service.

## What your bot is missing, separated by confidence

Your coin engine is already competitive. **About 150k coins per run, about 477 coins/s and about 1.7M coins per hour** (your 10-run measurement on 2026-10-12) is above the about 1.09M/hour that cookieafk advertises ([cookieafk.com, EN](https://cookieafk.com/en)). It is level with khm0612's best model at about 153.9k per run ([PROJECT.md, EN](https://github.com/khm0612/cookierun-classic/blob/main/docs/PROJECT.md)). At 477 coins/s and 150k per run, a full cycle including menus takes about 314 seconds, or about 11.5 runs per hour if lives allowed. The missing piece is XP. The bot is tuned for long, coin-heavy runs, buys Double XP without measuring what it returns, and never tries the short Episode 6 runs that the XP-focused tools all converge on.

The evidence splits three ways:

- **Verified facts (multiple sources or primary documentation):**
  - Classic launched June 25, 2026, and global accounts started from level 1.
  - The Korean Kakao build is a separate, older population.
  - Paid bots sell dedicated short-run EXP modes, with Episode 6 named explicitly.
  - Devsisters threatens permanent suspension under a Fair Play campaign, and the server enforces per-run coin caps.
- **Unverified claims:**
  - The 125M curve.
  - 10 XP per second and the 5x cap.
  - The 30k–55k Alchemist yield.
  - 41,000+ XP sets.
  - 17–18k XP potion-lab runs.
  - Carousel +30% and Melon Guitar +20%.
  - The August EXP nerf.
  - Every vendor's XP figure.
- **Inferences:**
  - Flat or proc-based XP makes short runs optimal.
  - Lotus Root Phantom is 연근요괴.
  - Lives, not run speed, limit XP farming without crystals.

The plan below turns this into measurements your bot can make. Every test respects the rule of never buying lives with crystals.

| Step | What to build or test | What it settles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Add result-screen OCR for EXP, with a settle-wait like balagux's. Log EXP, run seconds, score, episode, cookie/pet/treasures and boosts bought to records.csv alongside coins. | Turns every claim above into something you can check. Without this, nothing below can be judged. |
| 2 | With an identical setup, alternate 10 full runs against 10 short runs (stop jumping after N jumps, or exit at a fixed time). Compute **XP per life** and XP per minute. | Whether XP is mostly time-based (long runs win per life) or flat or proc-based (short runs win on both counts). |
| 3 | Repeat step 2 on Episode 6 against your current episode, using your best +EXP set (Bright Cookie, Carousel or Melon Guitar if owned, Lotus Root Phantom if the "XP party" holding effect exists in your collection). | Whether the Korean and Thai "Episode 6" consensus holds on the global build after the August changes. |
| 4 | Test four boost conditions: no boosts, Double XP alone, x2 Point Booster alone, and both together. | Which boost actually multiplies XP, and whether the 5x cap makes stacking wasteful. Drop any purchase that doesn't pay for itself. |
| 5 | Add a separate XP mode beside the coin mode, switched by a config flag, so coin farming is not degraded. | Lets you trade coins for XP deliberately rather than by accident. |
| 6 | Automate the three daily potion-lab plays, and add heart send and mailbox-life claiming within your real friend list. | Adds up to about 50k XP a day and more lives without crystals, alt accounts or bought hearts. |
| 7 | Keep and widen jitter in jump timing and tap coordinates, keep long breaks, and cap XP-mode runs per day. | Reduces the "same pattern" signal Korean users and LDPlayer warn about, and avoids extreme per-run or per-day totals of the kind behind the Banana coin-cap bans. |

## Conclusion

Fast 110s look like a numbers question, not a secret. A level curve of about 125M XP becomes reachable in four months once a run pays about 40k XP, and the Thai market has packaged that as short Episode 6 "EXP modes", heart farms and multi-instance cloud phones. The surprising part is that the most important number, XP per run on the global build after the August changes, has not been published in any language. Vendors know it and sell around it, so their 39,999 caps and 12-second rounds are the best clue to its shape.

That gives you an advantage no source has: an instrumented bot. Your coin side is done. One week of logging XP per life across run length, episode and boost combinations will tell you whether Classic's XP is time-based or flat per run, and that answer alone decides whether chasing 110 under the no-crystal-lives rule takes months or is effectively out of reach.
